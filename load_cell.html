Tractive Battery Design

340V system for powering our EV vehicle

Project Overview

This is probably the most challenging project I have worked on so far. I had to teach my self mechanical engineering concepts, convince the machine shop manager to let me use the mill, and did countless FEA simulations to ensure the load cell would not deform under my desired load of 400Lbs, while keeping the measurment points on the arms weak enough so that they would experience stress and strain that was measurable. This was a delicate balance that I had to achieve, and I am proud of the final design. I used a full bridge strain gauge configuration to measure the stress and strain in the 4 most sensitive parts of each axis. I scribed the positions for the strain gauges so that they were in identical position on both sides, and adhered them with cyanoacrylate glue. I then soldered the wires to the strain gauges and ran the wiring through small holes in the load cell to keep everything tidy.
